# Brief

_Last updated: 2026-11-02 · Phase 2 (design direction)_

## Purpose

A simple, serious photographic portfolio. Within the first moments a visitor
should understand that this is a photographer's site and feel the **wild
atmosphere** of the work. Within five minutes they should know whose work it
is and how to get in touch — through the About page.

## Audience

- Institutions (galleries, museums, festivals, publishers, curators)
- Artists
- Anyone interested in photography

This audience values seriousness and clarity over novelty. They will judge
the work, and the presentation should never get in the way of that judgement.

## Structure

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| **Home / Portfolio** | One continuous stream of tiled photographs. No text on the images. |
| **About** | The only place for words: bio, statement, contact, (CV / exhibitions later). |
| **Image view** | Opening a tile shows the photograph large. _Details: see open questions._ |

Navigation is minimal: name (→ home) and About.

## Content

- The owner has a large archive; the site shows a **curated selection**,
  not the archive. This keeps the stream strong and the repo within limits.
- Each image carries hidden metadata (alt text; optionally title, place,
  year) for accessibility and search engines — not displayed in the grid.

## Tone

Reference points: Hiroshi Sugimoto, Bill Henson. Underground fine art,
analog, timeless.

Serious · restrained · quiet interface · wild images.
Classy without being decorative. No visual effects that compete with the
photographs.

## Resolved in round 2 (2026-11-02)

- Tiling: equal tiles, no gaps (D-006) · Viewer: screen-size image on the
  colour field (D-008) · Background: one bold colour, blue direction (D-007)
- Singles and series share the wall (D-005) · English and Hungarian (D-009)
- Controls are large geometric shapes (D-010)
- Indigo, square, four columns (D-011) · About at the end of the wall (D-012)
- Conventions may be broken for the philosophy (D-013)
- No About entry, humble name Kopasz Márton, 2 phone columns, toned marks (D-014)
- No circle, plain-text contact, deliberate order, no motion (D-015)

## Open questions

1. **Series and singles:** how are they represented? Prototype offers
   "one tile that opens into the series" vs "whole series on the wall,
   separated by pauses of plain field".
2. **Exact field colour** (deeper than indigo; colour picker in prototype).
3. **Typeface** for the About text (seven examples in prototype).
4. **Triangles on desktop:** appear on hover, or always visible?
5. **About content:** final statement, bio, contact; long CV later.
